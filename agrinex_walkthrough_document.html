1. Executive Summary & Project Purpose

AGRiNEX is an AI-powered, IoT-integrated Digital Mirror of the Actual Farm. It resolves the disconnect between raw agronomic science, real-time microclimate sensors, APMC mandi trading economics, and practical on-farm operations.

Rather than providing generalized advice or standalone calculators, AGRiNEX anchors every decision to the specific micro-environment of a farmer's registered parcel of land—taking into account exact soil texture, current root-zone moisture deficit, crop phenological coefficient ($K_c$), seasonal evapotranspiration ($ET_0$), acreage, and market price trajectories.

🌾 Core Agricultural Value

  • Precision Irrigation: Eliminates water & power wastage by computing exact run-time minutes and optimal diurnal watering windows.
  • Multi-Method Farm Support: Accommodates 9 distinct irrigation methods including dual-method setups (e.g. root drip + foliar cooling).
  • Vision AI Diagnostics: Computer vision diagnosis for topsoil N-P-K deficiency and leaf pathogen identification.

📈 Economic & Market Intelligence

  • Live APMC Mandi Ticker: Real-time price discovery with modal price spreads and freight distance deductions.
  • What If I Wait? Decision Engine: Simulates cold-storage holding economics against post-harvest glut price rebound.
  • Fail-Safe IoT Hardware: Edge gateway with pump relay interlocking and automatic pressure safety relief.

5. Walkthrough of Every Single Workflow & Methodology

Module 1: Digital Twin Farm & Zone Spatial Mapping

Objective: Create a persistent geospatial model of the farmer's physical property partitioned into distinct micro-zones with unique soil types, crops, and micro-climates.

  • Step 1.1: Farmer inputs farm boundaries and GPS coordinates via the interactive Leaflet/Canvas mapping engine.
  • Step 1.2: Farmer subdivides land into production zones (e.g. Zone 1: Wheat 2.0 ac; Zone 2: Chilli 1.5 ac; Zone 3: Cotton 3.0 ac).
  • Step 1.3: Soil class (Sandy Loam, Clay, Black Cotton) and phenological stage are assigned to each zone.

Module 2: Soil Health & Vision AI Analysis

Objective: Assess topsoil organic matter, moisture retention, and N-P-K nutrient status using computer vision combined with chemical sensor telemetry.

  • Step 2.1: Farmer captures a close-up photo of topsoil (0–15 cm root zone) under uniform ambient light.
  • Step 2.2: The vision model analyzes colorimetric saturation (humic content indicator) and aggregate structure (soil porosity).
  • Step 2.3: The engine generates an organic soil restoration prescription (FYM, Jeevamrutha, or pH buffering amendments).

Module 3: Crop Recommendation Engine

Objective: Recommend optimal crop choices based on soil composition, annual rainfall, seasonal temperature, and APMC market demand.

  • Step 3.1: Multi-factor vector evaluation: [N, P, K, pH, Soil Type, Rain, Min/Max Temp].
  • Step 3.2: Evaluates candidate crops against historical ICAR agronomic thresholds and calculates expected yield and net revenue per acre.

Module 4: Precision Irrigation & Accurate Timing Engine (Upgraded)

Objective: Calculate exact irrigation duration in minutes, total gross water volume in Litres, and optimal diurnal time-of-day windows to prevent evaporative loss.

Key Enhancements Implemented in this Release:

  • Other / Custom Method: Farmers can specify custom methods (e.g. Solar Drip Jet, Perforated Pipe) with custom efficiency ($50\% - 99\%$) and custom flow rate in LPM.
  • Dual / Multi-Method Coordination: Primary root delivery (e.g. Drip) paired with secondary canopy cooling (e.g. Micro-Sprinkler), balancing runtimes to prevent soil waterlogging.
  • Seasonal ET0 & Diurnal Timing Analysis:
    • Summer (Zaid, $ET_0 = 7.2 ext{ mm/d}$): Optimal window 05:30 AM – 08:30 AM or 05:30 PM – 07:30 PM. Avoid 11:00 AM – 04:00 PM (saves 30–35% water loss).
    • Monsoon (Kharif, $ET_0 = 4.2 ext{ mm/d}$): Optimal window 06:00 AM – 09:00 AM. Avoid prior to rainfall showers.
    • Winter (Rabi, $ET_0 = 3.2 ext{ mm/d}$): Optimal window 08:30 AM – 11:30 AM or 03:00 PM – 05:00 PM to avoid frost shock.
  • Acreage & Flow Scaling: Volumetric scaling ($1.5 ext{ acres} = 6,070 ext{ m}^2$) maintaining the standard 15-minute baseline for Zone 2 Chilli.

Module 5: Plant Disease Diagnosis & Rx Treatment

Objective: Instant identification of fungal, bacterial, and viral foliar diseases with organic and chemical remedies.

  • Step 5.1: Leaf/stem photo ingested via smartphone camera.
  • Step 5.2: Neural network classifies pathogen (e.g. Chilli Leaf Curl, Early Blight, Powdery Mildew) with >90% confidence.
  • Step 5.3: Prescribes dual remedies: Organic (Neem oil, Trichoderma viride) and Chemical (Copper Oxychloride, Azoxystrobin) with precise dosage per litre and withholding intervals.

Module 6: Market Intelligence & Live Mandi APMC Ticker

Objective: Provide transparent pricing across regional agricultural markets to maximize farm-gate realization.

  • Step 6.1: Real-time APMC price aggregation showing Min, Max, and Modal prices.
  • Step 6.2: 7-Day price velocity tracking (+₹/quintal/week).
  • Step 6.3: Freight friction deduction (₹/km/quintal) to highlight the highest net profit destination.

Module 7: Farmer-Friendly Profitability Intelligence & "What If I Wait?" Simulator

Objective: Provide a plain-language profit calculation and simulate holding crops in cold storage.

  • Step 7.1: Simplified 3-step farmer input: Yield in Quintals/Bags, Selling Price, and Production Costs.
  • Step 7.2: Instant pocket profit (₹) and ROI percentage.
  • Step 7.3: "What If I Wait?" simulation computes storage fees (₹/bag/mo) and weight decay ($1.5\% - 3.5\%$) to deliver a clear recommendation: "SELL NOW" or "HOLD FOR HIGHER PRICE".

Module 8: Edge IoT Hardware & Actuation Subsystem

Objective: Physical execution of irrigation schedules with fail-safe relay interlocks.

  • Step 8.1: ESP32 Gateway receives start command with zone ID, duration, and delivery method.
  • Step 8.2: Energizes main pump relay and opens target solenoid valve while monitoring pulse flow sensors.
  • Step 8.3: Automatic countdown timer shutoff and safety pressure relief trip at 3.5 bar.

6. Agronomic Mathematical Formulas & Water Balance Engine

1. Daily Crop Evapotranspiration:
ETc = Kc × ET0
Where Kc = Crop Growth Stage Coefficient (0.85 – 1.25), ET0 = Seasonal Reference Evapotranspiration (mm/day).
2. Soil Moisture Deficit & Net Irrigation Requirement (NIR):
Deficit% = θ_FC - θ_current
NIR_mm = (Deficit% / 100) × Zr × ρ_soil × 1000
Where θ_FC = Target Field Capacity (60%), θ_current = Current Sensor Moisture (e.g. 32.1%), Zr = Effective Root Depth (m), ρ_soil = Soil Water Factor.
3. Gross Irrigation Requirement (GIR) & Volumetric Delivery:
GIR_mm = NIR_mm / η_irrigation
V_litres = GIR_mm × (Area_acres × 4046.86)
Where η_irrigation = Method Efficiency (0.55 for Flood, 0.90 for Drip, 0.95 for Subsurface Drip, or Custom η).
4. Operational Run Duration:
T_minutes = V_litres / (Q_LPM × ScaleFactor)
Where Q_LPM = System Discharge Flow Rate (LPM). Calibrated: Zone 2 Chilli (1.5 ac, Sandy Loam) = 15 minutes for Drip.
5. Pump Electrical Energy & Cost:
E_kWh = P_kW × (T_minutes / 60)
Cost_INR = E_kWh × Tariff_INR (Default: ₹4.50 / unit).
